UPI notifications on Android

Here’s how the automatic suggestion feature is planned to work.

A future Android app can watch payment notifications from the UPI apps you choose, then suggest an amount for you to review. You would choose the category before saving. Notification access is optional, and the app should explain clearly what it can read.
This website demo can’t listen to other apps on your phone. The current web version lets you add UPI, cash, or card expenses manually.
