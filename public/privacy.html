Privacy

Moonledger privacy information

This page explains what Moonledger stores and how it is used. Moonledger is an expense-tracking tool, not a bank and not a financial institution.

Guest mode and device storage

If you use Moonledger without signing in, your entries are stored in this browser only and are not uploaded to Firebase. They may be available to another person using the same browser profile. When you later sign in, you can choose to copy guest entries into your Google account; a local copy remains on this device.

Account and expense information

When you sign in with Google, Moonledger uses your Google account identity to provide your account. Expenses, budgets, recurring bills, subscriptions, private IOU records, and no-spend check-ins are stored in Firebase under your account identifier so they can sync across your devices. Expense entries and private IOUs are not shown to other Moonledger users.

If you paste a bank or UPI message, Moonledger parses the text in your browser on your device. The message is not sent anywhere by the parser. If you review and save the resulting expense, the saved expense fields are synced to your account.

If you opt into a savings circle, members can see your display name, monthly budget, and total spending for the selected month. Your individual expense entries remain private. You can choose whether to join a circle.

Device storage and notifications

Moonledger may store your selected theme, reminder preference, challenge start date, and offline app data in your browser on that device. Offline data remains on the device and synchronizes when the app reconnects, if offline storage was enabled. If you opt into push reminders, Firebase Cloud Messaging stores a device push token and time-zone preference under your account so the scheduled function can send evening and upcoming-bill reminders. You can disable reminders in the app or your device settings. Monthly recap images and CSV exports are generated in your browser.

Advertising and cookies

Moonledger currently does not load advertising. The public budgeting guide has an ad placement prepared but disabled. If advertising is enabled in the future, this page will be updated before ads are served with details about advertising cookies, data use, and available choices, including how to manage personalized ads through Google Ads Settings.

Third-party services

Google Sign-In and Firebase provide account authentication, database, hosting, and optional push notification services. Their processing is subject to Google’s applicable terms and privacy information. Circle members who opt in can see each other’s display names, monthly budget totals, and total spending. Private expense details stay private.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Moonledger’s features or service providers change. Check this page for the current version.

For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through Moonledger’s GitHub Issues page.
